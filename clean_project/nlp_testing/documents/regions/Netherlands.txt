Country: Netherlands
Major Cities: Amsterdam, Rotterdam, The Hague, Utrecht, Eindhoven
Waste System Type: Underground containers & Recycling centers (Milieuparken)
----------------------------------------

General Sorting Rules for Netherlands:

- PMD (Plastic/Metal/Drink cartons): Often collected together, sometimes sorted post-collection
- Paper: Blue bin or underground paper container
- Glass: Underground glass containers (Glasbak)
- Organic (GFT): Green bin (mostly outside city centers)
- Residual waste: Underground container (often requires a municipal card / afvalpas)
