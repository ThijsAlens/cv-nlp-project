Country: Spain
Major Cities: Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia, Seville, Zaragoza
Waste System Type: Color-coded street containers (Contenedores)
----------------------------------------

General Sorting Rules for Spain:

- Plastic/Metal/Cartons: Yellow container (Contenedor amarillo)
- Paper/Cardboard: Blue container (Contenedor azul)
- Glass: Green container (Iglú verde)
- Organic: Brown container (Contenedor marrón)
- Residual waste: Grey container (Contenedor gris)
