Document: Glass Sorting Rules (Fost Plus / Flanders)

WHAT IS GLASS WASTE?

This concerns transparent glass packaging. You must throw them into the glass bin or glass container. Separate white (colourless) glass from coloured glass.

POINTS TO NOTE regarding glass sorting:
- Empty glass bottles and jars completely.
- Lids and caps may NOT go into the glass bin; you must sort these with PMD.
- Do not leave anything behind around the glass bin (illegal dumping is punishable).

ALLOWED when sorting glass (May go into the glass bin):
- Transparent glass bottles (wine, beer, water, etc.).
- Glass jars (for example, for spaghetti sauce or vegetables).
- Glass flasks (for example, for perfume).

NOT ALLOWED when sorting glass (May NOT go into the glass bin):
- Heat-resistant glass (such as oven dishes or Pyrex).
- Flat glass (such as windows or mirrors).
- Crystal glass and opal glass (such as white or opaque bottles).
- Porcelain, ceramics, and terracotta (such as plates or coffee cups).
- Lamps (fluorescent bulbs or energy-saving bulbs).
- Drinking glasses (wine glasses or water glasses).
