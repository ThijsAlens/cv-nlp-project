Document: Sorting Rules for Residual Waste (Household Waste)

WHAT IS RESIDUAL WASTE?

Residual waste is all household waste that cannot be sorted into other waste streams, is not hazardous, and fits easily into a standard garbage bag.

ALLOWED (May go in the residual waste bag):
- Dirty or greasy paper and cardboard (for example, a greasy pizza box or dirty chip container).
- Hygienic waste (diapers, wet wipes, paper tissues, paper towels).
- Cat litter.
- Vacuum cleaner bags and their contents.
- Cooled ash and cigarette butts.
- Contaminated Styrofoam in small pieces.

NOT ALLOWED (Absolutely NOT allowed in the residual waste bag):
- Recyclable materials: PMD, clean Paper & Cardboard, and Packaging Glass.
- GFT (Vegetable, Fruit, and Garden waste).
- All types of light bulbs (incandescent bulbs, energy-saving bulbs, LED, fluorescent). These must go to Recupel or the recycling center.
- Ceramics, porcelain, mirrors, or flat glass. This is stone waste/bulky waste and must go to the recycling center.
- Drinking glasses or heat-resistant glass (such as Pyrex oven dishes). Take this to the recycling center.
- Electrical and electronic appliances (Recupel) and batteries (Bebat).
- Small Hazardous Waste (SHW) such as paint, chemicals, and aerosol cans.
- Needles and syringes.
- Construction and demolition waste.
