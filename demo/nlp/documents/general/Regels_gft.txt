Document: GFT Sorting Rules (Organic Waste / Vegetable, Fruit and Garden Waste) - Flanders, Belgium

WHAT IS GFT?

GFT stands for Groente-, Fruit- en Tuinafval (Vegetable, Fruit, and Garden waste). It is collected separately from other waste streams. In Flanders, GFT is collected in a brown bin or green bin depending on the intercommunale. Some areas also offer public compost points.

POINTS TO NOTE FOR GFT:
- GFT waste must be placed in the designated organic waste bin (usually brown or green, depending on your municipality).
- Do NOT use plastic bags to line the bin; use paper bags or compostable bags only.
- Drain excess liquid before placing food scraps in the bin to avoid odours.

ALLOWED (May go in the GFT bin):
- Vegetable and fruit scraps (peels, cores, leftovers).
- Coffee grounds, tea bags (without staple), and paper filters.
- Eggshells.
- Fresh and cooked food scraps (meat and fish in small quantities, depending on the intercommunale).
- Grass clippings, leaves, small branches (max 1 cm diameter), and garden waste.
- Paper kitchen towels and paper bags used to collect GFT.
- Cut flowers and houseplants (without pot).
- Compostable bags certified as OK compost HOME.

NOT ALLOWED (May NOT go in the GFT bin):
- Plastic bags or non-compostable packaging.
- Cat litter, dog faeces, or pet waste.
- Diapers or hygiene products.
- Ashes or cigarette butts.
- Large branches or tree trunks (take to the recycling centre).
- Oils or fats (dispose via the recycling centre or a dedicated fat collection point).
- Glass, metal, or any non-organic materials.
