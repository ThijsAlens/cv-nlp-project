Country: Italy
Major Cities: Rome, Milan, Naples, Turin, Palermo
Waste System Type: Door-to-door (Porta a porta) & Street bins
----------------------------------------

General Sorting Rules for Italy:

- Plastic/Metal: Yellow or Blue bins (varies by municipality)
- Paper/Cardboard: Blue or White bins (varies by municipality)
- Glass: Green bins
- Organic (Umido): Brown bins
- Residual waste (Indifferenziato): Grey bins
