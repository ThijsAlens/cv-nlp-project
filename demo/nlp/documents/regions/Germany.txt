Country: Germany
Major Cities: Berlin, Munich, Hamburg, Cologne, Frankfurt
Waste System Type: Bin-based (Tonnen) & Deposit system (Pfand)
----------------------------------------

General Sorting Rules for Germany:

- Packaging (Plastic/Metal): Yellow bin or bag (Gelbe Tonne / Gelber Sack)
- Paper/Cardboard: Blue bin (Blaue Tonne)
- Glass: Public glass containers sorted by color (Altglas)
- Organic: Brown bin (Biotonne)
- Residual waste: Black or Grey bin (Restmüll)
