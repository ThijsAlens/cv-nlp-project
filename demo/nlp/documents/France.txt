Country: France
Major Cities: Paris, Marseille, Lyon, Toulouse, Nice
Waste System Type: Color-coded bins (Poubelles)
----------------------------------------

General Sorting Rules for France:

- Recyclables (Plastic/Paper/Metal): Yellow bin (Bac Jaune)
- Glass: White or Green bin / public containers
- Residual waste: Green or Grey bin
- Organic (Biodéchets): Mandatory separate collection since January 2024 (brown bin or public compost point)
