Document: PMD Sorting Rules (Fost Plus / Flanders)

WHAT IS PMD?

PMD stands for Plastic packaging, Metal packaging, and Drink cartons. These are collected in the blue PMD bag.

POINTS TO NOTE FOR PMD:
- Empty or scrape bottles or other packaging completely.
- Flatten plastic bottles lengthwise and put the cap back on.
- Remove plastic films from trays or bottles and throw them into the bag separately.
- Do NOT stack packaging inside each other.
- Do not attach anything to the outside of the bag.

ALLOWED (This must go in the PMD bag):
- Plastic packaging: Plastic bottles, flasks, trays, tubs, containers (e.g. for butter or mushrooms), pots (e.g. yogurt), tubes, plastic films, and plastic bags. - Metal packaging: Beverage cans, food cans, food or cosmetic aerosol cans, aluminium trays and bowls, metal lids, caps and crown caps, and metal beverage capsules.
- Beverage cartons: Cartons for milk, fruit juice, soup, etc.

NOT ALLOWED (This must absolutely NOT go in PMD):
- Packaging with child-resistant closures (push-and-screw caps).
- Packaging of hazardous products (motor oil, pesticides, paint, varnish, glue, silicone).
- Aerosol cans of hazardous products.
- Batteries and electrical or electronic devices (including vapes and smartphones).
- Hard plastic packaging larger than 8 litres (such as large buckets).
- Styrofoam.
- Gas cylinders or camping gas.
- Other objects made of plastic or metal that are not packaging (such as toys, garden hoses, buckets).
