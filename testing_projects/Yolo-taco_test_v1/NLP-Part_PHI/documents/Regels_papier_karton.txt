Document: Paper and Cardboard Sorting Rules (Fost Plus / Flanders)

WHAT IS PAPER AND CARDBOARD?

This includes dry and clean paper and cardboard packaging and printed matter. Pack it in a sturdy, closed cardboard box or tie it together.

ALLOWED (May go with paper and cardboard):
- Paper bags and cardboard boxes (folded or flattened).
- Newspapers, magazines, and leaflets.
- Books and writing or printing paper.
- Egg cartons (made of cardboard).
- Cardboard food packaging (e.g., cornflakes boxes), provided they are empty and dry.
- Cardboard rolls for toilet paper or kitchen roll.
- Paper envelopes (including those with windows).

NOT ALLOWED (MAY NOT GO with paper and cardboard):
- Dirty or greasy paper and cardboard (such as a greasy pizza box or dirty chip container). This must go with the residual waste.
- Tissues, paper towels, and napkins. This must go with the residual waste. - Wallpaper. This must go in the residual waste or at the recycling center.
- Aluminum foil.
- Photos.
- Plastic wrap around magazines or advertisements (remove this first; the plastic wrap must go in the PMD bin).
